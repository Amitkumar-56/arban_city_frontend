Welcome to Kohinoor Central Park

Kohinoor Central Park is a premium residential township located in Hinjewadi, Pune, developed by Kohinoor Group. Spread across 13.5 acres, the project features 12 towers rising up to G+25 floors and offers 2 BHK, 3 BHK homes and exclusive duplex residences with deck views. The 2 BHK homes range from 725 to 808 sq. ft. carpet area and start from ₹86 Lacs onwards, while the 3 BHK residences range from 884 to 1,087 sq. ft. carpet area and start from ₹1.07 Cr. onwards. For homebuyers researching the kohinoor central park 2 bhk price, the project website lists ₹86 Lacs onwards as the starting price for 2 BHK residences. Designed around an urban forest concept, Kohinoor Central Park Hinjewadi combines green boulevards, vertical gardens, sky terraces and landscaped green spaces with more than 50 curated amenities. Key amenities include dual luxury clubhouses, an elevated nature walk, biophilic co-working space, Miyawaki Botanical Garden, skating rink with amphitheatre, bar deck with water views, kids’ pool, mini theatre, reflexology walking trail, spa, sauna and jacuzzi, along with eco-smart sustainable systems. Strategically positioned in Hinjewadi Phase II within Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, kohinoor central park provides convenient access to major employment and connectivity zones. Hinjewadi IT Hub is listed at 5 minutes, TCS at 7 minutes, Capgemini Hinjewadi at 9 minutes and KNORR-BREMSE Technology Center at 13 minutes, making the location suitable for professionals working across the technology corridor. Connectivity is supported by the Doehler Metro Station at 3 minutes, Mumbai-Pune Highway at 15 minutes and Pune-Mumbai Expressway at 15 minutes. The website also lists Ryan International Academy at 5 minutes, IIBM at 16 minutes, Ruby Hall Clinic at 14 minutes, Sajeevani Multispeciality Hospital at 12 minutes and Jupiter Hospital at 29 minutes. While the website mentions access to schools, malls/markets and hospitals, it does not provide specific nearby railway stations, airport or mall names, so these details have not been added. For buyers comparing kohinoor central park pune with other Hinjewadi residences, its 13.5-acre setting, nature-focused planning, extensive amenities and proximity to major commercial hubs are key location advantages. The kohinoor central park hinjewadi address places the development within one of Pune’s established technology and business destinations, while buyers specifically searching for kohinoor central park 2 bhk can consider the listed 725–808 sq. ft. carpet-area homes starting from ₹86 Lacs onwards.

Set on a sprawling 13.5-acre plot in Hinjawadi Central, Kohinoor Central Park offers residents unparalleled convenience and connectivity to key city landmarks.
